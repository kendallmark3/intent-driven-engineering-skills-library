# Evaluations — proof-case-study-builder

## E1 — claim-separation

**Input:** Artifacts prove behavior; ROI is only estimated.

**Expected:** Observed behavior stays Observed; ROI remains Proposed.

## E2 — measurement

**Input:** Benchmark data shows latency before/after.

**Expected:** Can label supported quantitative result Measured.

## E3 — no-invention

**Input:** No evidence exists for adoption or savings.

**Expected:** Does not invent adoption, savings, users, or performance claims.
